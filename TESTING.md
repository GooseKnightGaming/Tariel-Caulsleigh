# Sunday test checklist (phone or Chromebook)

Everything below was checked in a headless browser (no real screen, about 1 frame a second, no sound), never by a person.
Please note what *feels* wrong: speed, sizes, volume, anything confusing. Tick or scribble as you go.

## First, the basics
- [ ] Title screen: does it say **Continue** if you've played before? Does **New game** wipe it?
- [ ] On a phone: stick (left thumb), look-drag (right side), buttons (E, Jump, Run, Q, F, J, I, Map, pause). Is the stick too twitchy or too slow? Are buttons the right size?
- [ ] Frame rate on your phone. The game starts light (no shadows, lower resolution). Pause menu: try Shadows and Picture.
- [ ] Sound (N turns it off): wind, footsteps, birds by day, crickets by night, tavern murmur and a wandering harper. Too loud? Annoying?

## Walking and looking
- [ ] Tariel and Thiah (Q swaps): does the walk animation look right? Does Tariel's coat swing?
- [ ] Can you walk through barrels, crates, the handcart, stalls, the goat pen's rails? (Barrels/crates/stalls should block you; the pen's rails are open air.)

## Talking (E beside a person) and the journal (J)
- [ ] Choices by number key or tap. Does a tap on the talk box continue when there are no choices?
- [ ] Journal: purse, threads ("What ties together"), notes.
- [ ] Elar's first scene, the harper's song (1 dinar), the money-changer (both directions).

## Games and trials
- [ ] **Threot** (round table in the tavern): hold dice 1-3, roll, stand. Fair? Fun?
- [ ] **Toasts** (long table): sip / gulp / bluff. Draft game, my invention: replace it with yours.
- [ ] **Archery** (market, west edge): crosshair drift speed. Too fast / slow? Prizes?
- [ ] **Fistfight** (talk to a villager or stallholder): odds fair? Leave mid-game costs 2 dinar.
- [ ] **Sword drill** (as Tariel, talk to a guard): is the call speed readable? One trait point a day (K).
- [ ] **Brawl** (first night around 21:18-21:57): press F near the two men.

## The three nights
- [ ] Sleep at the tavern as Thiah, night 1 -> morning news. Again for night 2 (house with the open door and Cob's pen become searchable) and night 3 (lamps gutter if you're in the tavern around 21:36-22:12).
- [ ] After night 3, walk to Shappel-ont'-Laik: the placeholder end card.
- [ ] Close the page mid-game and reopen: does Continue put you back correctly?

## What I did not or could not check
- Real-time feel of anything (animation, archery drift, drill timing).
- Sound by ear. Phone performance. A real touch screen (I only simulated touches).
- Whether the tavern's barrels/crates look right (I only saw the market props and the pen).
